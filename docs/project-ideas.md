# Project ideas to build together

Personal projects to add to the site, saved from planning in October 2026.

1. **Interactive apartment tour.** Builds on the Blender mini apartment. Export it as `.glb` and show it with Three.js so visitors can look around. Click a piece of furniture or decor to see why it was chosen, plus the inspiration and color palette.
2. **Palette from a photo.** Upload a photo and get back a color palette and mood board. A small, personal take on the image work at Treering.
3. **Food diary map.** A pretty map of places I've eaten, with photos and ratings.
4. **Generative prints.** In-browser art inspired by my studio art printmaking. Each visit creates a new piece you can download.

Lead project: **Mini apartment in Blender.** Waiting on renders (PNG/JPG) and a `.glb` export (Blender: File → Export → glTF 2.0, `.glb` format).
